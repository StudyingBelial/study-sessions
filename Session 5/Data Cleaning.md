# Overview
Data cleaning is simply the process of preparing raw data by detecting and then correcting errors, which then can be further analyzed down the line. This step is quite often the first step in any preprocessing pipeline as raw data is often noisy and inconsistent, directly impacting any calculations further out.
Cleaning data is important in any context, whether it be for Machine Learning or for any statistical analysis task, where interpretation of data is vital, and with noisy raw data proper relationships and conclusions may be impossible to be found. The main objective of Data Cleaning is to improve the quality of the data by correcting for errors and inconsistencies.

## Why is Data Cleaning important?
Intuitively we may understand that Data Cleaning is beneficial but how exactly is it beneficial? For one thing it improves efficiency and time savings as the errors and inconsistencies are found and dealt with in the beginning making the process down the line more straight forward.
There is also the matter of consistency, where one of the main things done here is that inconsistency in the scale or the units of data is dealt with such that the data becomes uniform and consistent.

## What we want to achieve?
The main points to achieve in the pursuit of a higher quality data by data cleaning are:
1. Accuracy
2. Completeness
3. Consistency
4. Uniformity /  Standardization
